# KCL composition principles

Use these questions alongside the Style Bible in [art-direction.md](art-direction.md). Iterate concept, real material and composition together; do not reserve drawing quality for the final polish.

| Decision | Ask | Record in spec/brief | Failure to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Concept | What visual relationship makes the theme specific? | `imagery.tension` (free-form concept sentence), motifs and roles | Generic occult inventory unrelated to the subject |
| Hierarchy | What reads first, second, then close up? | `principles.hierarchy`, element rank/size/value | Every medium-sized object competes |
| Balance | Where is the dominant mass, and what counterbalances it? | `principles.balance` | An arbitrary black disk or empty region added by habit |
| Alignment | Which edges, contours, axes or centres belong together? | `principles.alignment`, anchors | Near-tangencies, detached ornament, gratuitous crossing lines |
| Proximity | What is one visual assembly, and what must read separately? | `principles.proximity`, layers | Many independent generated stickers without shared shape |
| Flow | How do gaze, gesture, type, contrast and space connect priorities? | `flow`, ranked stations | A drawn red guide that the actual image does not support |
| Rhythm | How do large/middle/small, dense/quiet and repeated/changed alternate? | `principles.repetition` | Equal rows of stars/figures filling gaps; rhythm reduced to dots |
| Contrast | Which differences carry the concept? | `principles.contrast` | Everything has the same sharpness, tone and density |
| Negative space | What shape does the empty area make? What does it protect? | `principles.whitespace` | Filling intentional air to meet a paper percentage |
| Color | What leads in value, temperature, saturation and area? | `color`, Style Bible | Default red/blue signal or forced grayscale damages the illustration |
| Material | Which fine features must survive at display size? | asset plan and proof | Primitive ornament, muddy masks or coarse halftone destroy craft |

## Shape and character

Read the actual silhouette, gesture, gaze, hair/cloth direction and negative shapes between limbs. Place key geometry from meaningful landmarks rather than the rectangular image box. Protect the face, hands and identity details unless their occlusion is the concept. A poor pose requires a new crop, image edit or better source, not additional diagonal lines.

A geometric frame may echo the gesture, oppose it, be interrupted by it or remain absent. Small exact ticks can contrast with irregular ink; a thick exact ring is not inherently more convincing than a fine engraved contour.

## Density at multiple levels

Let the subject carry detail where its drawing deserves attention. Keep structural lines legible but subordinate where needed. Put ornament in related clusters and material texture on the surfaces that produce it. Review the composition at thumbnail, intended size and close crop: each scale should reveal a useful different level of information.

A large complex assembly can provide richness with only a few compositional elements. A thousand identical dots can still be visually empty. Element count, image count and paper percentage cannot make that distinction.

## Proof and iteration

Start with a rough mass/route sketch, test uncertain material, then refine the spec using actual shapes. The proof may be a sketch, a partial material composite or a final-render draft. Record what is decided and what remains provisional. Honor user-requested approval; otherwise proceed within existing authorization. Reopen only decisions affected by new evidence or user direction.
