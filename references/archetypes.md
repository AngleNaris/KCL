# Composition structures

Choose for the content and mix deliberately. These are starting relationships, not prescribed containers or inventories. Preserve established project structure on local edits.

| Structure | Organizing idea | Material opportunity | Narrow adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scroll Bands | successive scenes with varied mass and continuity | one meaningful material/gesture recurring across scenes | retain narrative order; recompute crop/spacing; allow height to grow |
| Icon Center | intentional symmetry or central concentration | a coherent engraved ornament or ceremonial assembly | fold peripheral detail inward or crop it; keep focal contours |
| Asymmetric Grid | large image against editorial hierarchy | image, paper fragment and live type sharing edges | reduce columns; reflow prose; preserve the image's dominant gesture |
| Celestial Chart | relations between a focal body and observations | engraved apparatus with precise vector paths/ticks | simplify peripheral observations before shrinking the main instrument |
| Anatomical Atlas | whole object plus meaningful detail studies | drawn anatomy, cloth or structural sections | stack details in reading order; keep labels adjacent |
| Specimen Archive | multiple related but distinct specimens | coherent asset family with varied silhouettes | fewer columns; preserve comparative scale where meaningful |
| Surreal Collage | unexpected relation between unlike objects | generated/sourced assemblies with shared print language | keep the main relation; remove subordinate fragments selectively |
| Editorial Spread | image, display type and text act across a spread | crops, folds, negative shapes and local paper/ink | convert spread to ordered sequence; reset body text measure |
| Sequential Diagram | states or scenes imply time | separate material states and restrained transitions | stable sequence or static key frames; avoid tiny unreadable panels |

For type-led or abstract work, imagery may be absent. For a dense archive, several subjects/centres may be essential. For a quiet plate, most ground may remain visible. Record why the chosen structure serves the concept.

Series cohesion comes from drawing, color and typography; variation comes from scale, mass, gesture, crop and relations. Do not manufacture variation by swapping a circle for an arch while repeating everything else.
